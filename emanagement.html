> WHAT_IS_EMANAGEMENT

A client-side finance tracker because I refuse to trust my personal financial data on some random company's cloud server. I ditched the soul-crushing spreadsheet look and over-engineered a visually heavy, interactive dashboard that runs entirely offline. No backend, no accounts, no slop.

> WHY_I_BUILT_IT

Honestly, I built this because my spending habits are absolute garbage. I bleed money on stupid things and desperately needed a system to yell at me before I go broke. It's built for myself, and for anyone else who's similarly reckless with their cash and needs a brutal, visual reality check.

> DATA_ARCHITECTURE

Everything lives locally inside your browser's LocalStorage. Maximum privacy, zero latency, and zero dependency on crappy APIs. It brutally recalculates financial states and manipulates the DOM in milliseconds. If you clear your browser data, you lose everything. Deal with it.
