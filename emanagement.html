> WHAT_IS_EMANAGEMENT

A premium, modern personal finance tracker running entirely on the client side. I ditched the boring, traditional spreadsheet look and built a visually heavy, interactive dashboard that doesn't even need a backend.

> DATA_ARCHITECTURE

Everything lives in your browser's LocalStorage. Maximum privacy, zero latency. It recalculates financial states and renders the DOM in milliseconds.
